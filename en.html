Privacy Policy

Data Saver Browser

Last updated: September 2, 2026

Provider: Two Kids Lab

Introduction

Two Kids Lab ("we" or "us" or "our") operates the "Data Saver Browser" mobile application

(the "App"). This document informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use,

and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

Browsing History and Bookmarks

All browsing history and bookmarks recorded in the App are stored locally on your device only.

  • Browsing history data is never transmitted to our servers or any third party
  • Bookmarks are never transmitted to our servers or any third party
  • We cannot access, view, or retrieve your browsing history or bookmarks
  • When you uninstall the App, all browsing history and bookmarks are deleted from your device

Internet Communication

The App requires internet access to function as a web browser and manage per-site

savings settings.

  • Regular web browsing (HTTP/HTTPS traffic) is direct communication between your device

and the websites you visit. We do not capture, intercept, or log this traffic

  • Tracker and script blocking is performed locally on your device
  • Our servers do not process or store your web traffic

Advertising

The free version of this App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

AdMob may automatically collect information such as your Advertising ID for the purpose

of delivering personalized advertisements based on your interests.

User Messaging Platform (UMP)

The App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage consent for ad delivery.

  • A consent form may appear on your first launch or based on your location
  • Your consent status is stored locally on your device and is never sent to our servers
  • You can change your consent status at any time

Opt-out of Personalized Ads

You can disable personalized advertising in several ways:

  • From your device settings, go to Settings → Google → Ads → Reset Advertising ID
  • Or select "Remove Ads" (a one-time in-app purchase) within the App

For more information about Google's privacy practices, see:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

Permissions

The App requests the following permissions:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to browse websites and communicate with online services
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to determine network type (Wi-Fi vs. mobile)

These permissions are essential for the App's core functionality. We do not request

permissions for the camera, microphone, location, or other sensitive features.

Local Data Storage

Savings Statistics

  • Data savings are recorded by category (trackers, scripts, images, etc.)
  • Aggregate statistics (by site, by time period) are stored in a local database on your device
  • This data is never transmitted externally

Per-Site Settings

  • For each domain (e.g., amazon.com), your chosen savings mode is stored locally
  • Your settings are automatically applied on your next visit to that site
  • These settings remain on your device and are never uploaded

Purchase Information

In-app purchases to remove ads are processed through Google Play. Google handles all

payment information and credit card data. We never see or store your payment information.

Only whether you have made a purchase is recorded locally and in your Google Play

purchase history.

Third-Party Disclosure

We do not disclose your information to third parties, except as required by law.

The App uses Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform, which are services

operated by Google LLC. Google's privacy policies apply to any data processed by these services.

Data Deletion

Uninstalling the App will delete all of the following data from your device:

  • Browsing history
  • Bookmarks
  • Per-site savings settings
  • Data savings statistics

Alternatively, you can delete app data by going to Settings → Apps → Data Saver Browser

→ Storage → Clear Cache or Delete Data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant

changes via app updates or notifications.
