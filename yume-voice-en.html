Privacy Policy

Yume Voice (ゆめボイス)

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Provider: Two Kids Lab

Introduction

Two Kids Lab ("we") sets out below how the Android app "Yume Voice" ("the App") handles user information.

The App is designed to be used by a child together with their family. Privacy protection was the starting point of its design.

The App does not collect or transmit any information.

The App has no ability to connect to the internet. It does not request the Android INTERNET permission at all, so even if we wanted to, it is technically impossible for the App to send any data outside your device.

Audio recordings

Audio recorded by the App (the goal declaration, the daily "I did it!" recordings, and so on) is stored only on your device.

  • Audio files are never transmitted to our servers or to any third party
  • We cannot listen to, download, or otherwise access the contents of any recording
  • Audio files are stored in the app's private internal storage and cannot be read by other apps
  • Uninstalling the App deletes all audio files from your device

Recorded data

The following data is also stored only on your device.

  • Goal titles and progress
  • Date and time of each "I did it!" record
  • Mood selections
  • Stickers earned
  • App settings (whether Outing Mode is on)

Information we do not collect

The App never collects any of the following.

  • Name, date of birth, address, phone number, or email address
  • Photos or camera images
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage statistics or interaction logs

Advertising

The App displays no advertising whatsoever. No advertising software (such as AdMob) is embedded in the App. Accordingly, no child's information is ever used for advertising purposes.

Analytics

The App uses no analytics tools, no crash reporting tools, and no other usage measurement tools of any kind.

Device permissions

The App uses exactly one permission.

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) — used to record the child's voice. Recording occurs only when the record button on screen is pressed; the microphone is never active at any other time. Recordings are saved on the device.

The internet permission is not requested.

Sharing recordings

The App can export a "Growth Drama" — the recordings joined into a single audio file. A parent can then send that file to family members using apps such as LINE or email.

  • Sharing happens only when a parent explicitly chooses to do so
  • Sharing uses the standard Android share sheet and does not pass through our servers
  • This feature sits behind the parental check (a simple arithmetic question)
  • How the audio is handled after sending is governed by the privacy policy of the app used to send it (LINE, etc.)

Children's privacy

The App is intended to be used by children. We place particular importance on protecting children's privacy and follow these principles.

  • We do not collect personal information from children
  • We do not ask children to enter personal information
  • We do not disclose or share children's information with third parties
  • We place no external links and no advertising on screens intended for children
  • High-impact actions — changing settings, deleting data, and exporting audio — sit behind the parental check

Because the App neither collects nor transmits data, it does not engage in any activity that constitutes "collection of personal information" under the United States Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Deleting your data

You can delete your data yourself at any time.

  • The parental settings screen in the App can delete all data, including audio files
  • Deleting a goal also deletes the audio and records associated with that goal
  • Uninstalling the App removes all data from your device

Because we hold no data, there is no need to send us a deletion request.

Backups

The App disables Android's automatic backup feature. Recorded audio and records are never automatically copied to cloud storage such as Google Drive.

Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy as necessary. If we do, we will post the revised policy on this page and update the "last updated" date.
