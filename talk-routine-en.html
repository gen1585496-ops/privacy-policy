Privacy Policy

Talk Routine

Last Updated: September 7, 2026

Provider: Two Kids Lab

Introduction

Two Kids Lab (hereinafter "We") provides the Android app "Talk Routine" (hereinafter "the App").

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

Voice Recordings

All voice files recorded by parents in this app are stored only on your device.

  • Your voice recordings (question prompts and praise voices) are never sent to our servers
  • Your voice recordings are never sent to third parties
  • We cannot access, hear, download, or view your voice files
  • When you uninstall the app, all voice files are permanently deleted from your device

Task and Sticker Data

All task information, completion records, and sticker data are stored only on your device.

  • Task definitions (name, time, day of week, repeat settings)
  • Task completion history
  • Sticker book collection data
  • Reward settings

This data is never transmitted to our servers or any third party.

Internet Communications

The app uses internet access solely for Google AdMob ad delivery.

  • Your task information, voice files, and personal data are never sent over the internet
  • AdMob communications are limited to ad serving purposes, per Google's policies

Advertising

The app displays banner ads powered by Google AdMob.

Ads appear on the routine editing screen only.

That screen is where a parent enters task names, sets times, and records voice prompts.

  • No ads are shown on the alarm screen, the praise animation, the sticker book, or the task list
  • Ads never appear on screens a child operates

Treatment for Children

Because the app is intended to be used by children, we tag our AdMob requests as

child-directed.

  • No interest-based (personalized) ads are served
  • The app is not used for remarketing or tracking-based advertising
  • Ad content is restricted to the 'General audiences' rating

Opting Out of Ads

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings:

(Settings → Google → Ads → Advertising ID: Reset or Delete)

For Google's complete privacy policy, see:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

Permissions Explained

The app requests the following permissions:

  • android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO: To record voice prompts and praise
  • android.permission.SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: To trigger alarms at scheduled times
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS: To display task notifications
  • android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE: To keep audio playback stable
  • android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK: For audio service operation
  • android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: To restore active tasks after device restart
  • android.permission.WAKE_LOCK: To keep screen on during alarm playback
  • android.permission.USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT: For full-screen alarm display
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: For haptic feedback on task completion
  • android.permission.INTERNET: For AdMob ad delivery
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: To check network connectivity

The app does NOT request location, camera, contacts, calendar, or other sensitive permissions.

Local Storage

Voice Files

  • Recorded voice files are stored in the app's private directory (`/data/data/com.talkroutine.app/files/`)
  • This directory is isolated from other apps (sandbox protection)
  • Files are automatically deleted when you uninstall the app

Task and Sticker Data

  • Stored in a local SQLite database managed by Room ORM
  • Kept on your device until you uninstall the app
  • You can delete this data anytime via Settings → Apps → Talk Routine → Storage → Clear Cache/Clear Data

Third-Party Data Sharing

We do not share your information with third parties except as required by law.

Google AdMob is a service provided by Google LLC and is subject to Google's privacy policies.

While we are responsible for managing how we use these services, any information sharing with

Google follows Google's privacy policies, not our choice.

Data Deletion

Uninstalling the App

Uninstalling Talk Routine permanently deletes all of the following from your device:

  • All voice files (prompts and praise recordings)
  • Task definitions and settings
  • Task completion history
  • Sticker book data
  • Reward settings

Clearing Data Without Uninstalling

You can delete data through your device settings:

1. Open Settings

2. Select "Apps" or "Application Manager"

3. Find and select "Talk Routine"

4. Select "Storage and Cache"

5. Tap "Clear Cache" (partial deletion) or "Clear Data" (complete deletion)

Privacy Policy Changes

We may update this policy as needed. We will notify you of significant changes through

app updates.
